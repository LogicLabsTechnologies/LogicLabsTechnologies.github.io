Introduction

LogicLabs Technologies develops mobile applications designed to provide useful utility features.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our applications.

Find My Car

Find My Car is a utility application that helps users remember where they parked their vehicle.

The application uses location functionality to allow users to save and view their parking location.

Location Information

Find My Car may access your device's location when you use the parking-location feature.

Location information is used to provide the core functionality of the application, including saving and displaying the location where you parked.

Information Collection

We only collect or process information that is necessary to provide the functionality of our applications.

The exact information handled by each application may vary depending on its features.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be shared with third-party services only when required for application functionality, advertising, analytics, legal requirements, or other purposes clearly disclosed to users.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by our applications.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the relevant application functionality or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Children's Privacy

Our applications are not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
