Privacy Policy

LogicLabs Technologies

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Introduction

LogicLabs Technologies develops mobile applications designed to provide useful utility features.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our applications.

ParkPin

ParkPin is a utility application that helps users remember where they parked their vehicle. It is designed as an offline-first application to maximize your privacy.

The application uses location functionality to allow users to save and view their parking location on a map.

Location Information

ParkPin requires access to your device's location data to provide its core functionality, such as pinpointing your current parking location and calculating distance for navigational assistance.

All location processing happens entirely on your device. We do not transmit, upload, or share your location data with our servers.

Information Collection & Storage

Any data you create or save within ParkPin (such as saved parking spots and preferences) is stored entirely locally on your device.

We do not maintain any cloud databases or external servers that collect this information.

Third-Party Services

While ParkPin operates mostly offline, we utilize OpenStreetMap to display map tiles. When you view the map, your device may send standard network requests to OpenStreetMap's servers to fetch the map imagery.

ParkPin does not use any third-party analytics trackers or advertising SDKs.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Since all your data is stored locally on your device, we do not have access to it, nor do we share it with third parties.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by our applications. Because data remains on your device, your device's own security mechanisms act as the primary protection.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained only on your device. You can delete all your saved parking data simply by uninstalling the application or clearing the application's data in your device settings.

Children's Privacy

Our applications are not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the support information provided on our website.
