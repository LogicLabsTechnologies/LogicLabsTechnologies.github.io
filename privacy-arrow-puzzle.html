Privacy Policy

Arrow Puzzle: Tap Away

Effective Date: September 15, 2026

Summary: Arrow Puzzle: Tap Away is an entirely offline game. We deeply respect your privacy and do not collect, store, or share any personal information. You can play with complete peace of mind.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Arrow Puzzle: Tap Away. This Privacy Policy is designed to transparently inform you about our data practices when you use our mobile application. We have built this game with privacy in mind, fully adhering to the requirements of global privacy regulations and Google Play Store policies.

2. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal data or usage data.

Arrow Puzzle operates entirely locally on your device. The app does not require an internet connection, nor does it possess the permissions to access the internet. Any progress, unlocked levels, settings, or high scores you achieve are stored exclusively within your device's local secure storage.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

We do not integrate any third-party SDKs for advertising (such as AdMob or AppLovin), analytics (such as Google Analytics or Crashlytics), or telemetry tracking. Because we do not collect any data, we do not share any information with third parties.

4. Device Permissions

To ensure maximum privacy, our app requests the absolute minimum permissions required to run on the Android operating system. We do not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or external storage files beyond what is strictly necessary to save your game state locally.

5. Data Security

Because no data leaves your device, the risk of data interception or breaches from our servers is completely eliminated. Your local game data is protected by the inherent security sandboxing features of the Android operating system. Please note that if you uninstall the app or clear its app data, your game progress will be permanently lost.

6. Children's Privacy

Arrow Puzzle is safe, accessible, and suitable for all ages, including children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are a parent or guardian, you can be assured that this game is completely offline and provides a secure environment for your child.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy if our practices change (for example, if we introduce new features in the future). Any changes will be reflected directly on this page, and the "Effective Date" at the top will be updated. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
