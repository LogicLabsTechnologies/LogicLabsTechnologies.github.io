Privacy Policy

Arrow Puzzle: Tap Away

Effective Date: September 15, 2026

Summary: Arrow Puzzle: Tap Away uses third-party advertising services to support development. While your game progress is stored locally, these ad networks may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Arrow Puzzle: Tap Away. This Privacy Policy is designed to transparently inform you about our data practices when you use our mobile application. We have built this game with privacy in mind, fully adhering to the requirements of global privacy regulations and Google Play Store policies.

2. Information Collection and Use

Local Game Data: Your core gameplay progress, unlocked levels, and high scores are stored exclusively within your device's local secure storage and are not transmitted to our servers.

Advertising Data: To keep our game free to play, we use third-party advertising networks. These networks may collect certain information automatically when you play the game, including your IP address, device type, operating system, and unique Advertising Identifiers.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

We integrate third-party SDKs to display advertisements. These companies may use cookies, device identifiers, and similar technologies to collect information about your device and your interactions with the game in order to provide targeted advertisements and analyze ad performance.

For more information on how these ad networks collect and use your data, please review their respective privacy policies:

  • AppLovin Privacy Policy

4. Device Permissions

To function properly and load advertisements, our app requests the INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions. We do not ask for access to your camera, microphone, precise location, contacts, or external storage files beyond what is strictly necessary to save your game state locally.

5. Data Security

Your local game data is protected by the inherent security sandboxing features of the Android operating system. Data collected by our third-party advertising partners is governed by their own stringent security and privacy practices. While we strive to partner with reputable networks, we cannot guarantee the absolute security of data transmitted over the internet.

6. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. However, third-party ad networks used in our app may collect device identifiers to serve non-personalized contextual ads to younger audiences. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information outside of these parameters, please contact us.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy if our practices change (for example, if we introduce new features in the future). Any changes will be reflected directly on this page, and the "Effective Date" at the top will be updated. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
