Privacy Policy for Arrow Escape

Effective Date:

Logic Labs ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you play our mobile application, Arrow Escape (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

When you use the App, we may collect certain information automatically, including:

  • Device Information: Such as your mobile device ID, model, manufacturer, operating system, and version information.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the App, including session length, levels completed, and interactions with in-game features.

Note: Arrow Escape does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email, or phone number.

2. Third-Party Advertisements (Unity Ads)

To keep the App free to play, we use Unity Ads to serve advertisements. Unity Ads may collect and process certain data to provide personalized and non-personalized ads, monitor ad performance, and prevent fraud.

Data collected by Unity Ads may include:

  • IP address
  • Device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA)
  • In-game location and actions (to measure ad viewability)

For more information on how Unity collects and processes your data, please review the Unity Privacy Policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information primarily to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App's functionality and performance.
  • Serve relevant advertisements through our third-party ad networks.
  • Analyze usage trends to enhance the user experience.

4. Data Retention

We retain usage data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. Data collected by third-party services (like Unity) is subject to their respective data retention policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Arrow Escape does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.We does not store personal data at our server as game works fully local.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
