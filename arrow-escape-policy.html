Privacy Policy for Arrow Escape

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and shared when you play Arrow Escape.

1. Information Collection and Use

Arrow Escape is designed to provide a fun puzzle experience. While your core game progress (stars, levels, and unlocked themes) is saved locally on your device, our app requires an internet connection to provide certain features, such as displaying advertisements.

2. Third-Party Advertising and Analytics

We use third-party advertising companies (such as Google AdMob) to serve ads when you visit our app. These companies may use information about your device and your visits to this and other apps in order to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you.

  • Advertising IDs: Third-party ad networks may collect your device's Advertising ID to serve personalized ads.
  • Analytics: We may use third-party analytics tools to help us measure traffic and usage trends for the app. These tools collect information sent by your device, including the device type, operating system, and crash reports.

For more information on how Google collects and uses data, please visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Local Game Data

Your game progress is saved locally on your device using local storage. This specific gameplay data never leaves your device and is not accessible by us. If you uninstall the app, your local progress will be permanently deleted.

4. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices (for example, integrating new ad networks). We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at the developer email provided on our Google Play Store listing.
